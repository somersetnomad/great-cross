# The Great Cross

An interactive 3D study of the Great Cross at the heart of John Dee's four Enochian Watchtowers.

**Live site:** https://somersetnomad.github.io/great-cross/

## What it does

- **Explore** the 36 squares of the Great Cross for the Air, Water, Earth and Fire tablets in 3D.
- **Unveil** the cross in five stages: the sealed matrix, the Three Holy Names, the six Seniors, the King, and a tarot study layout.
- **Learn** where the tables come from, and what studying them does and doesn't offer, in a plain-language guide.
- **Practise** with a twelve-week programme of daily study and contemplation, with progress tracking and supporting videos.
- **Share** what you find. The share studio turns entries from a knowledge base into posts for X, Bluesky, Threads, LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, or into quote images in four sizes. Every post and image links back to the site.

Links can open the site on a particular tablet or square:

- `#air`, `#water`, `#earth` or `#fire` opens that tablet.
- `#water-c6r7` opens the Water tablet with column 6, row 7 selected.

## Sources

The letters, Holy Names, Kings and Seniors follow John Dee's corrected Great Table of 1587, as transcribed in Stephen Skinner and David Rankine, *The Practical Angel Magic of John Dee's Enochian Tables* (Golden Hoard Press, 2004). The planet colours and the tarot placement are later, Golden Dawn–style overlays and are marked as such on the site.

## Running it

The whole app is one static `index.html` that loads Three.js and Google Fonts from CDNs, so it needs no build step. Open the file in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server.
